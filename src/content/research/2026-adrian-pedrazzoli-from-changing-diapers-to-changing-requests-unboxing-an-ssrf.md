New features are the most fertile ground for bug hunters. In this turbo talk I walk through how an endpoint in the "Unboxing" feature of a major e-commerce platform turned out to be vulnerable to SSRF: the first sniff at the feature, the validation bypass, and the road to the report in the bug bounty program.
