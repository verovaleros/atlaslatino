What happens after submitting a bug bounty report.
This talk explores the full process from a triager’s perspective: how reports are evaluated, what common mistakes affect their acceptance, and which best practices can make a difference. We will also look at how AI is influencing report quality and the validation process.
A practical perspective on how decisions are made on the other side—and how to maximize the impact of your findings.
Presented in Spanish.
