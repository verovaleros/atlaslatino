The most recent supply chain attacks don't exploit vulnerabilities in code. They exploit the pipeline. Nobody treats it as an attack surface, and that's exactly how Shai-Hulud compromised 14k+ secrets without touching a single application. Through a live demo you'll see the attack from the attacker's perspective and walk away with a practical framework to evaluate and strengthen your own pipelines.
