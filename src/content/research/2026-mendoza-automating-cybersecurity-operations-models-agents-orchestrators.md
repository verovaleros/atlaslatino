This training introduces cybersecurity automation using AI models, agents, orchestration, and MCP (Model Context Protocol). It focuses on building flexible security workflows where models help analyze alerts, logs, and security data; agents decide what actions to take; and orchestrators connect tools, data, and people into reliable processes.

The course emphasizes practical design patterns and hands-on examples, including low-code workflow engines and human approval steps. By the end, participants are expected to be able to design scalable automations that reduce repetitive work, improve response time, and integrate AI into security operations while keeping human oversight in place.
