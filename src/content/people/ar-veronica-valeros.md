I am Veronica Valeros, a cybersecurity researcher from Patagonia, Argentina, with over a decade of experience in threat intelligence, malware traffic analysis, and data-driven security. I work as a senior researcher and co-director of the Stratosphere Research Laboratory at the Czech Technical University in Prague, where I also teach the Introduction to Security course, both in a physical class and as a large-scale MOOC. I have presented at international conferences, including Black Hat, EkoParty, Botconf, and Virus Bulletin, and I serve on review boards for Black Hat and academic events. I am the co-founder of MatesLab hackerspace in Argentina and the Independent Fund for Women in Tech. I deliver intensive, hands-on training in network traffic and malware analysis, drawing on over 18,000 hours of network traffic investigation experience. Since 2025, I have been pursuing a PhD in Computer Science and AI at the Czech Technical University in Prague while completing a master's degree in Intelligence and Security Studies at Liverpool John Moores University.
