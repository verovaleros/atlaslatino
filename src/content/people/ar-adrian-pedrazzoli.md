I currently serve as a Senior Product Security Engineer at Function Health. Previously, I served as a Senior Product Security Engineer at Salesforce, where I've executed over 100 security assessments across Agentforce AI, Heroku, and Mulesoft since 2021. I created and lead an internal offensive security interest group bringing together 20+ cross-functional team members monthly, fostering knowledge sharing across Salesforce's worldwide operations. In my previous roles at Banco Galicia, I conducted comprehensive security audits and penetration testing, ensuring PCI compliance for over 5 million banking clients.

As co-founder of Bug Bounty Argentina, we've grown our community from 20 founding members in 2020 to over 1,000 active researchers today. Our organization has hosted live hacking events with platforms like HackerOne and YesWeHack, and I co-founded the Bug Bounty Village at Ekoparty, Latin America's largest security conference.

My bug bounty research has uncovered critical vulnerabilities including RCEs in AT&T and Meta, SSRF and IDOR vulnerabilities in MercadoLibre, and SQL injections in BMW and Ford. I've discovered over 150 XSS vulnerabilities, +120 IDORs, and access control issues across 48 programs, with 20 being Fortune 500 companies. My work has earned hall of fame recognition from Google, Meta, Amazon, PayPal, Uber, Microsoft, and MercadoLibre, among others.

Personally, I love diving deep into hands-on technical work performing actual assessments, but I'm also adept at threat modeling, architecting security solutions, and leading community initiatives. While my strengths are web applications, APIs, and AI platforms, I have experience across the security spectrum from financial services compliance to advanced exploitation techniques.

In my free time, I speak at security conferences including Ekoparty, Universidad Tecnológica Nacional, and BugCon Mexico. I actively participate in bug bounty programs across platforms like HackerOne, YesWeHack, Google, Meta, Microsoft, and Intigriti.
