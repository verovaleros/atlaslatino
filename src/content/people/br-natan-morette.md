Natan Morette is a Penetration Test Manager, vulnerability researcher, and cybersecurity instructor for Brazil's national Hackers for Good initiative, where he mentors students in offensive security across the country. He began his career as a help desk analyst and moved through infrastructure roles before discovering his passion for cybersecurity. Natan has discovered and published multiple CVEs and actively supports students in identifying and disclosing their own, especially in open-source projects with meaningful social impact.
